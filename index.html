FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Kickbacks make money?

Retailers pay a commission when you shop through an offer. We share most of that with you and keep a small portion. We don't charge you for the core service.

Do I need a new credit card?

No. Kickbacks works with the cards you already have and stacks on top of the rewards they already earn.

When will it launch?

We're opening access in waves. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when your spot is ready.

Is my data safe?

We use read-only connections, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and never sell your personal or purchase information.
